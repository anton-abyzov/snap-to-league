Miami.
Office cups, school leagues,
and even hackathon fellows.
and they still
use a piece of paper to
track all the results.
That's what inspired us to create
a solution
to convert it all into a
beautiful league management software.
Let's take a look.

or do it live.

A second AI pass checks the
extraction.
Review the teams and scores before
publishing.

and we could read it out.

the bracket,

statistics
