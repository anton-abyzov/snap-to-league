This is a phone photo of a sports competition record: a handwritten whiteboard, a paper scoresheet, a printed bracket or a screenshot of a group chat. Extract it for a league management app.

Rules:
- Copy team names exactly as written; keep the same spelling every time a team appears.
- A match with a score is "played". A match with a time, a date, a field or no score is "scheduled".
- stage is "group" unless the board labels the match a final, semifinal, quarterfinal or knockout game.
- group is the group label if the board has one (for example "A"), otherwise null.
- when and venue are copied as written ("Sun 10am", "Field 2"), otherwise null.
- sport is your best guess from the board (soccer, futsal, basketball, volleyball, padel, other).
- Put anything you could not read confidently, and anything that contradicts itself, in "uncertain" as a short sentence a tournament organizer would understand.
- Then compute the group standings from the played group matches only: 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw.
- Do not run any commands.

Answer ONLY with JSON in exactly this shape:
