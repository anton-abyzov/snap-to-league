## Research and sending record

For each prospective message, record the organization, intended role, public contact source, event evidence URL, checked date, why the workflow fits, the exact approved copy and any prior contact. Keep recipient information in a private operational record, not this public campaign document.

Choose one message and one ask. The first-touch drafts deliberately use one proof link; the longer partner letter includes the demo and Devpost for a recipient who needs background. Do not attach a large video or send three alternatives to the same person.

Measure delivered messages, positive replies, introductions and completed trials. Track refusals and suppress further contact. Do not present opens or clicks as customers, and do not claim a relationship until the organization actually agrees.
