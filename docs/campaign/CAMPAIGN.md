# Snap to League — campaign copy

**Editorial plan, not scheduled posts.** Publish only after the corresponding final video, demo URL and account identity are checked. Lead with a real scoresheet becoming a reviewed competition; sponsor integrations support that story.

## Positioning

**Audience:** organizers of office cups, school leagues, gaming brackets and informal competitions.
**Problem:** results already exist on paper or in a screenshot; sharing them requires another round of entry.
**Promise:** start with the photo, review the result, give people a competition they can follow.
**CTA:** try [Snap to League](https://snap.easychamp.com).

Primary hook: **“Still tracking scores on paper?”**
Payoff: **“Keep playing. Let the photo do the paperwork.”**

Avoid “first,” “perfect,” “instant,” “millions,” guaranteed time savings, guaranteed wins and lifetime access. The public demo currently depends on a development Mac and tunnel. Podiums share on Snap; EasyChamp handles supported game competitions.

## Seven-day sequence

Day 1 starts when the verified launch assets are ready, not automatically on this document's date. Reuse each approved cut across suitable channels without repeatedly posting the same asset to one account.

| Day | Asset / angle | Destination | What to measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main founder-led demo + vertical proof | YouTube, Short, Reel; concise LinkedIn launch | Views, available completion metrics, tagged demo visits |
| 2 | One readable paper→review→standings sequence | X or a short follow-up post | Link visits and questions about the workflow |
| 3 | Show one real correction before saving | Short educational cut, if captured | Saves, replies and completed demo attempts |
| 4 | Bracket source beside its reviewed result | Reel or carousel | Which competition formats viewers request |
| 5 | Actual Read out snippet and when it is useful | Short / LinkedIn technical note | Audio-feature questions, not inferred adoption |
| 6 | Cup-stacking event context plus a labeled sample podium | Story or short clip | Interest in placement contests; disclose Snap-only sharing |
| 7 | What worked, what failed and the next improvement | LinkedIn + repository update | Reproducible feedback and return visits |

Use only analytics actually available. UTM tags identify campaign links; they do not by themselves establish conversion tracking. Report raw observations and sample size before claiming a lift. No paid spend, outreach messages or automatic scheduling is included in this plan.
